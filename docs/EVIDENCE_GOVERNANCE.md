# AMAL OS — Master Islamic Evidence Governance & Knowledge Graph Specification

## 1. Executive Mandate & Islamic Epistemology

AMAL OS is architected to protect the sacred integrity of Islamic revelation and scholarly tradition. The platform operates on a foundational epistemological rule:

> **AI is an explanatory retrieval assistant; it is never a source of religious authority, it cannot issue fatwas, and it must never fabricate, distort, or extrapolate religious texts.**

Every Quranic verse, Hadith narration, and scholarly citation rendered in AMAL OS is mathematically verifiable, grounded in canonical sources, and audited against an immutable evidence graph.

Islamic knowledge within AMAL OS is structured strictly across six authority tiers:

```text

├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 2: AUTHENTIC SUNNAH (Sahih & Hasan Hadith)        │

├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 6: AI SYNTHESIS (Natural language synthesis only)  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Tier 1 — Quranic Revelation**:
   - Arabic text verified against the King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Quran (Medina Mushaf, Uthmani script).
   - Vetted authoritative translations (Saheeh International, Hilali-Khan, Yusuf Ali, Abdel Haleem, Clear Quran).
   - Absolute epistemic certainty (`Qat'i al-Thubut`).
2. **Tier 2 — Authentic Hadith (Sunnah)**:
   - Canonical collections: *Kutub al-Sittah* (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami` at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah) plus Muwatta Malik and Musnad Ahmad.
   - Rigorous grading attributes: Sahih (Sound), Hasan (Good). Da'if (Weak) narrations are strictly tagged and restricted from normative rulings.
3. **Tier 3 — Classical Tafsir & Juristic Consensus (*Ijma'*)**:
   - Canonical commentaries: Tafsir Ibn Kathir, Tafsir al-Qurtubi, Tafsir al-Tabari, Tafsir al-Sa'di.
   - Foundational juristic compendia representing the four recognized Sunni schools (*Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali*).
4. **Tier 4 — Recognized Contemporary Jurisprudence**:
   - Resolutions from international Fiqh academies (International Islamic Fiqh Academy - IIFA, European Council for Fatwa and Research - ECFR, AMJA).
5. **Tier 5 — Curated Editorial Content**:
   - Practical daily habits, educational guides, reflection prompts written by qualified scholars and educators.
6. **Tier 6 — AI Synthesis**:
   - Dynamic natural language explanations generated by governed AI models. **AI synthesis is non-authoritative and must always link directly to Tier 1–4 nodes.**

## 4. Medina Text Hash Immutability

To guarantee that the Quranic text can never be altered or tampered with:
- Every Surah and Ayah is hashed using SHA-256 upon initial loading from the Medina Mushaf baseline.
- The hashes are permanently recorded in the `quran_hash_ledger` database table.
- A background integrity cron verifies the active database content against the cryptographic ledger on a continuous schedule.
- Any modification, deletion, or unintended glyph alteration triggers an immediate **SEV-0 Alert** and freezes API writes.

## 5. Multi-Scholar Grading & Ikhtilāf Plurality

AMAL OS does not enforce artificial homogenization on matters of legitimate scholarly divergence (*Ikhtilāf*):

### 5.1 Plurality Representation
- Where classical schools disagree on valid juristic branches (e.g., details of prayer postures, calculation methods, fasting exceptions), the evidence graph models multiple parallel valid positions.
- Each position is linked to its respective juristic evidence and identified with its classical school (*Madhhab*).
- AI agents are instructed: *"Present the consensus where consensus exists; present the recognized differing positions with their respective evidences respectfully where diversity of scholarship exists."*

### 5.2 Two-Person Rule for Evidence Publication
- Any new Hadith grading adjustment or contemporary scholarly entry requires independent sign-offs from **two distinct credentialed scholars** before transitioning from `PENDING_REVIEW` to `PUBLISHED`.
- Scholar review records capture digital signatures, timestamps, and rationales in the `scholar_evidence_reviews` table.

## 6. Citation Auditor & Zero-Tolerance Fabrication Policy

Before any AI-generated response containing religious evidence is delivered to an end-user:

1. **Citation Extraction**: The output is parsed by the Citation Extraction Engine to identify Quranic citations (`[Surah Name ayah:verse]`) and Hadith citations (`[Collection, Hadith Number]`).
2. **Cryptographic Validation**: Each citation is looked up in the primary `evidence_graph` database using composite keys `(surah, ayah)` or `(collection, number)`.
3. **Fidelity Verification**: The cited text in the response is compared against the canonical Arabic and approved translations using token-overlap scoring.
4. **Enforcement Action**:
   - If a cited reference does not exist: **Immediate Reject & Regenerate**.
   - If after 2 attempts a valid citation cannot be produced: **Strip religious claim** and output safe guidance or escalate to human scholar triage.
5. **System Invariant (`INV_EV_01`)**: Zero fabricated or hallucinated citations are permitted in AMAL OS production output. Citation accuracy benchmark must remain $\ge 99.0\%$.
