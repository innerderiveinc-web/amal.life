## 4. PHASE 3 — TEXT & REFERENCE VERIFICATION

Raw acquired files undergo rigorous four-tier validation before ingestion into the database:

### 1. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) QA
- Any scanned or imaged edition must undergo dual-pass neural OCR with proofreader validation.
- Target Character Accuracy: $\ge 99.8\%$.
- Any page with confidence below 99.0% is quarantined for manual transcription review.

### 2. Diacritic & Orthographic Policy
- Original classical Arabic diacritics (*Tashkeel*) and Uthmanic script orthography are preserved identically for rendering.
- A parallel normalized token stream (stripped diacritics, unified hamzas) is indexed exclusively for semantic search and fuzzy alignment.

### 3. Granular Reference & Structural Mapping
Every work is decomposed into its native hierarchy:
- **Quran:** `Surah (1-114) : Ayah (1-6236) : Word Offset`.
- **Hadith:** Universal numbering (Bukhari Fath al-Bari numbering, Muslim Abd al-Baqi numbering), Sanad-Matn boundary tokens.
- **Fiqh:** `Kitab -> Bab -> Fasl -> Mas'alah -> Legal Argument`.
- **Volume & Page Index:** Verified directly against the physical published edition.

### 4. Bitwise Validation & Conflict Detection
If two editions diverge:
- The system flags the difference as an editorial variant (*Ikhtilaf Nuskha*).
- Both variants are recorded with their respective editor attributions. The relied-upon critical edition remains primary.

## 5. PHASE 4 — SCHOLARLY CLASSIFICATION & EPISTEMIC TAXONOMY

Each verified passage is categorized across the 12 Governed Knowledge Domains:
$$\text{Revelation} \longrightarrow \text{Hadith} \longrightarrow \text{Tafsir} \longrightarrow \text{Fiqh Position} \longrightarrow \text{Spiritual Guidance}$$

### Classification Attributes:
- **Tradition & Madhhab:** Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali, or General Sunni.
- **Relied-Upon Status:** Is it the adopted position of the school (*Al-Mu'tamad*) or an unadopted view?
- **Scope of Ruling:** Universal obligation (*Fardh/Wajib*), Recommended (*Mustahabb*), Permissible (*Mubah*), Disliked (*Makruh*), or Prohibited (*Haram*).
- **Consensus Flag:** Does this ruling enjoy verified scholarly consensus (*Ijma'*), or is it an area of legitimate juristic difference (*Ikhtilaf Mashru'*).
