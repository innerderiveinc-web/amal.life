## 1. Governance Policy & Evaluation Standard

In accordance with the **AMAL OS Zero-Fabrication Policy**, no Islamic claim may be presented as verified or authoritative unless its source, work, edition, canonical reference, cryptographic provenance artifact hash, and required scholarly reviews have been fully validated.

### Verification Levels
- **LEVEL 7 (PRODUCTION PUBLISHED)**: Meets all 7 criteria: Source Identified + Reference Verified + Provenance Hash Verified + License Approved + Editorial Review Approved + Scholar Review Approved + Production Published.
- **LEVEL 6 (SCHOLAR REVIEWED)**: Completed technical, editorial, and multi-scholar verification; pending release batching.
- **LEVEL 5 (EDITORIALLY APPROVED)**: Academic and editorial review passed; awaiting resident scholar sign-off.
- **LEVEL 4 (PROVENANCE & ATTRIBUTION VERIFIED)**: Cryptographic SHA-256 hash sealed; text normalized; attribution checked.
- **LEVEL 3 (REFERENCE VERIFIED)**: Reference validated against canonical chapter and verse/hadith numbering bounds.
- **LEVEL 2 (SOURCE IDENTIFIED)**: Source work, author, and edition cataloged.
- **LEVEL 1 (CANDIDATE)**: Discovered or ingested; unverified.
- **LEVEL 0 (UNKNOWN / QUARANTINED)**: Unverified, broken provenance, or license unapproved. Strictly blocked from retrieval.
