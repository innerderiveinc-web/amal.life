## 6. Known Scope Boundaries in V0.5

1. **Feature Flag Control (`ISLAMIC_AI_ENABLED`):**  
   Pre-production deployment maintains `ISLAMIC_AI_ENABLED=false` by default until external scholar roster provisioning is complete. All test scripts enable and exercise the runtime deterministically.
2. **Research vs. Production Quarantine:**  
   Unevaluated community suggestions or candidate research files are stored in the Research Vault and are cryptographically isolated from production retrieval.
3. **Autonomous Fatwa Ban:**  
   The AI runtime is architecturally prohibited from issuing personalized legal rulings (`direct_ruling_allowed: false`). Sensitive cases are routed to the human Two-Scholar Triage Desk.
