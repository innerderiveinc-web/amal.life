# AMAL OS — SCHOLAR PLATFORM & JURISTIC GOVERNANCE

## 1. Operating Thesis

Human scholarly oversight is not a marketing veneer or an afterthought; it is an architectural foundation of AMAL OS.

> **AI must never act as an independent Mufti or issue unvetted personal religious rulings (Fatwas).**

Where questions enter personalized juristic complexity (Class D), the system transitions seamlessly from automated intelligence to human pastoral guidance.

---

## 2. Scholar Network Architecture

### 1. Verified Scholar Profiles (`verified_scholars`)
Every scholar participating in the network undergoes rigorous verification:
- Institutional credentials (degrees from recognized Islamic institutions such as Al-Azhar, Islamic University of Madinah, Darul Uloom, etc.).
- Provenance of Ijāzah and specialization areas.
- Multidimensional skills matrix: Fiqh of Worship, Islamic Finance, Family & Marriage, Youth Pastoral Care, Bioethics, Mental Health Chaplaincy.

### 2. The Two-Person Rule (`INV_04`)
For sensitive Class D determinations:
- **Lead Jurist:** Reviews case context, evaluates evidence, and drafts a pastoral response.
- **Peer Reviewer:** A second distinct jurist independently audits the response before release.
- **Constraint:** Lead Jurist $\neq$ Peer Reviewer. Both must be authenticated members of the scholar registry.

### 3. Preservation of Ikhtilāf (Plurality)
When legitimate historical differences exist among classical schools of thought:
- Disagreements are preserved and celebrated as mercy and breadth in the Shariah.
- The system never forces an artificial consensus or imposes a single sectarian stance.

---

## 3. Scholar Consultation Economy & Escrow

- **Consultation Modes:** Written Clarification, Detailed Pastoral Dossier, Family Guidance.
- **Transparent Fee Split:**
  - 85% directly to the participating scholar.
  - 15% platform fee for infrastructure and administrative maintenance.
- **Escrow Guarantee:** User fees are held in escrow and disbursed only upon completed response delivery and verification.
- **Decoupled Authority:** Scholar theological ranking and assignment are strictly decoupled from commercial metrics, app usage volume, or social popularity.

---

## 4. Privacy & Case Isolation

- Case context is strictly scoped to the specific inquiry.
- Scholar responses are delivered privately to the user's secure inbox.
- Responses never leak into general LLM pre-training sets or become visible to unrelated users.
- Institutional admins and family members cannot view personal scholar cases without explicit user consent.
